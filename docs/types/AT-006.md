## Definition

Engages in multi-turn natural language conversation with users to answer questions, provide information, or assist with tasks through dialogue.

## How It Works

Maintains a conversation history (messages list), passes it with each turn to an LLM, and returns the model's reply. May be augmented with retrieval, tools and memory to ground responses and extend capabilities.

## Strengths

Natural user interface; broad applicability; easy to deploy via chat platforms; can combine knowledge from pre-training with real-time retrieval.

## Limitations

May hallucinate facts; conversation context window limits length; difficult to maintain consistent persona across very long sessions; latency can degrade experience.

## Typical Examples

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini chat, customer support bots, HR FAQ assistants, health symptom checkers.

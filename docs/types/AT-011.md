## Definition

Autonomously searches the web and databases, synthesises information from multiple sources, and produces structured research reports with citations.

## How It Works

Receives a research topic or question; decomposes it into sub-queries; uses web search, academic database and tool APIs to retrieve sources; reads and extracts key facts; synthesises findings into a coherent report with citations; iterates if gaps remain.

## Strengths

Covers vast information spaces quickly; produces cited, structured output; reduces research time dramatically; can re-run to update findings.

## Limitations

May surface low-quality or biased sources; fact-checking is imperfect; cannot access paywalled content; synthesis quality depends heavily on retrieval quality.

## Typical Examples

Perplexity deep research, OpenAI deep research mode, Gemini deep research, custom academic literature review agents.

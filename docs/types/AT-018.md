## Definition

A specialised agent fine-tuned or configured for a specific industry with domain-specific tools, knowledge bases and compliance constraints.

## How It Works

Built by combining a foundation model with domain-specific fine-tuning or RAG over industry corpora (medical literature, legal case law, financial regulations); equipped with industry-specific tool integrations (EHR systems, legal databases, trading platforms); governed by compliance rules baked into guardrails.

## Strengths

Deep domain expertise; meets industry-specific regulatory requirements; more accurate than a general assistant in its domain; reduces need for expert consultants.

## Limitations

Expensive to build and maintain; regulatory approval may be required; fine-tuning can degrade general reasoning; domain knowledge goes stale.

## Typical Examples

Nabla healthcare assistant, Harvey legal AI, BloombergGPT financial assistant, industrial maintenance agents.

## Definition

Continuously monitors systems, logs and network traffic for anomalies and threats, escalating or responding automatically to detected incidents.

## How It Works

Ingests streams of security telemetry (logs, metrics, alerts); applies anomaly detection, signature matching and LLM-based reasoning to triage alerts; correlates events across sources; determines severity; either automatically remediates low-risk events or escalates high-risk ones to human analysts with a detailed report.

## Strengths

Continuous coverage; faster triage than human analysts; correlates signals across large data volumes; reduces alert fatigue with intelligent prioritisation.

## Limitations

High false-positive rates can erode trust; autonomous remediation carries risk of service disruption; adversaries can probe agent behaviour to evade detection; requires access to sensitive systems.

## Typical Examples

Darktrace AI analyst, Microsoft Sentinel AI, custom SOC agents on SIEM data, cloud security posture agents.

## Definition

Automates a defined sequence of steps in a business or technical process by integrating with multiple tools and APIs in a structured pipeline.

## How It Works

Receives a trigger (schedule, event or user request); executes a predefined or dynamically planned sequence of tool calls, data transformations and conditional branches; produces a structured output or side-effect such as a sent email or updated record.

## Strengths

Reliable, auditable automation; integrates heterogeneous systems; reduces manual effort; deterministic when the workflow is fixed.

## Limitations

Rigid to scope changes without workflow redesign; debugging cross-system failures is complex; may require significant integration work upfront.

## Typical Examples

Invoice processing pipelines, CI/CD orchestration agents, data ETL agents, HR onboarding automation.

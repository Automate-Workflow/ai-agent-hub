## Definition

Executes purchases, negotiations or financial transactions autonomously on behalf of a user, with configurable authorisation controls and spending limits.

## How It Works

Receives a commercial intent (buy, negotiate, refund); verifies the action against a user-defined policy (spending limit, allowed merchants); interacts with payment APIs, e-commerce platforms or negotiation counterparties; confirms execution and reports back; logs all transactions for audit.

## Strengths

Automates tedious purchasing workflows; can optimise for price or speed; enforces spending policies automatically.

## Limitations

Irreversible financial actions require extremely high reliability; fraud and social engineering risk; regulatory compliance complexity; trust and liability concerns.

## Typical Examples

Amazon agent purchasing, travel booking agents, automated procurement bots, forex execution agents.

## Definition

An agent that responds immediately to stimuli without deliberate planning or memory, optimised for speed and simplicity in well-defined environments.

## How It Works

Implements a direct stimulus-response mapping (rule engine, neural policy, or lookup table). On each tick, reads the current input and immediately produces an output with no internal deliberation cycle. State is entirely in the environment or the most recent observation.

## Strengths

Extremely low latency; simple to implement; deterministic and auditable; robust since there is no complex internal state to corrupt.

## Limitations

Cannot handle tasks requiring multi-step reasoning or memory; brittle outside the design envelope; cannot learn from experience without external adaptation mechanisms.

## Typical Examples

Real-time game bots, reflex-based robot controllers, simple IVR systems, high-frequency trading signal generators.

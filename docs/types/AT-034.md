## Definition

An agent that explicitly plans and reasons before acting, trading speed for thoroughness and correctness in complex or novel situations.

## How It Works

Maintains an explicit world model. Before each action, runs a planning or reasoning algorithm (search, logical inference, LLM chain-of-thought) over the model to identify the best action sequence. Executes the selected action and updates the world model based on the outcome.

## Strengths

Produces higher-quality decisions for complex tasks; can explain its reasoning; adapts plans when outcomes differ from expectations; handles novel situations.

## Limitations

High latency per step; computationally expensive; planning quality depends on world model accuracy; can get stuck in loops if planning fails to converge.

## Typical Examples

Multi-step reasoning agents, theorem-proving agents, strategic planning agents, medical diagnosis reasoning agents.

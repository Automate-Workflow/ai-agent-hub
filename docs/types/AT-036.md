## Definition

Operates for extended periods on complex multi-step tasks with minimal human intervention, managing its own planning, tool use and memory.

## How It Works

Sets its own sub-goals from a high-level objective; maintains a persistent task graph with completed, in-progress and pending nodes; executes steps using tools and code; writes intermediate results to durable storage; monitors for blockers and either works around them or escalates; resumes seamlessly after interruption.

## Strengths

Can complete ambitious projects autonomously over hours or days; reduces total human time investment; learns from failures within a run.

## Limitations

Error accumulation over long horizons; difficult to monitor and correct mid-task; risk of unrecoverable states; unpredictable resource consumption; alignment risks from unsupervised goal pursuit.

## Typical Examples

Devin autonomous software engineering, AutoGPT long-running tasks, AI-driven overnight research pipelines.

## Definition

Integrates reasoning across text, images, audio and potentially video in a unified cognitive loop, treating all modalities as first-class inputs.

## How It Works

A unified multimodal model (or a composition of modality-specific encoders feeding a shared reasoning core) processes inputs from multiple modalities simultaneously. The reasoning loop can interleave modality-specific tool calls (image generation, audio synthesis) with text reasoning to produce a cross-modal output.

## Strengths

Solves genuinely cross-modal tasks (describe what you see and hear); enables richer interfaces; can generate multimodal outputs; strongest available modality is always leveraged.

## Limitations

Largest inference cost among architecture types; modality alignment errors (misattributing audio to wrong visual element); limited multimodal training data for rare modality combinations; complex prompt engineering.

## Typical Examples

Gemini 1.5 Pro multimodal reasoning, GPT-4o omni model, Reka multimodal agents, video understanding research agents.

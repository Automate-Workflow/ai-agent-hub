## Definition

Answers questions by retrieving relevant documents from a knowledge base and grounding responses in retrieved content using the Retrieval-Augmented Generation pattern.

## How It Works

Embeds the user query and searches a vector store or keyword index for the most relevant document chunks. Retrieved passages are injected into the LLM prompt as context. The LLM synthesises an answer grounded in the evidence, reducing hallucination risk.

## Strengths

Keeps responses factual and up-to-date without full fine-tuning; supports citations; knowledge base can be updated independently of the model.

## Limitations

Retrieval quality limits answer quality; chunking strategy significantly affects results; latency from retrieval adds to response time; long documents may exceed context.

## Typical Examples

Enterprise Q&A bots over internal wikis, legal document assistants, medical literature search agents, customer support agents over product docs.

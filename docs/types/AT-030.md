## Definition

An AI agent controlling a physical robot body, integrating perception of the physical world with language reasoning and motor action planning.

## How It Works

Sensor inputs (cameras, LiDAR, touch, proprioception) are processed by perception models to generate a world-state representation. An LLM or planning module reasons over the state and goal, generates a high-level action plan, which is translated into low-level motor commands by a robot controller.

## Strengths

Bridges language AI and physical world; can follow natural language instructions for physical tasks; enables generalisation across diverse environments.

## Limitations

Physical safety risk; sim-to-real gap; latency between perception and action must be low; robot hardware is expensive; reliability requirements are extremely high.

## Typical Examples

Google DeepMind RT-2 (robotic transformer), Figure AI humanoid with OpenAI LLM, Boston Dynamics Spot with language guidance.

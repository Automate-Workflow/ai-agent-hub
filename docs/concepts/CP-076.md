## Definition

Systematically gathering, cross-checking, reconciling, and summarizing evidence across heterogeneous documents, databases, and APIs.

## Why It Matters

Multi-source synthesis underpins market diligence, literature review, real-estate valuation, and security investigation agents.

## How It Works

Agents issue targeted sub-queries across distinct corpora, track provenance per claim, flag conflicts, and assemble cited reports.

## Example

Synthesizing public filings, comparable property sales, and zoning codes into a cited valuation memo.

## Common Mistakes

Concatenating conflicting source excerpts without resolving discrepancies or attributing provenance.

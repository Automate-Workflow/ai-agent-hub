## Definition

Tripartite evaluation diagnostic assessing context relevance, groundedness (faithfulness), and answer relevance across RAG pipelines.

## Why It Matters

Measuring all three links pinpoints whether RAG errors come from poor retrieval or ungrounded model generation.

## How It Works

Evaluators such as TruLens and Ragas score each retrieved-context and answer pair along the three independent dimensions.

## Example

Diagnosing that low answer scores stemmed from weak chunk retrieval rather than generator prompt issues.

## Common Mistakes

Checking final answer fluency alone without verifying whether retrieved context actually contained relevant facts.

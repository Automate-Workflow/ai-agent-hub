# CP-047: orchestrator and workers

![orchestration](../../assets/icons/orchestration.svg) ![intermediate](../../assets/badges/intermediate.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `orchestration` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

A hierarchical pattern in which a central orchestrator decomposes complex requests, dispatches parallel or sequential worker agents, and synthesizes results.

## Why It Matters

Orchestrator-worker separation keeps each worker focused on narrow context while allowing central quality integration.

## How It Works

The orchestrator dynamically plans sub-tasks, invokes specialized subagents as tools or jobs, and inspects returned summaries.

## Example

A deep research coordinator spawning separate literature, documentation, and competitor analysis workers.

## Common Mistakes

Passing raw massive sub-agent traces back into the orchestrator rather than distilled summaries.

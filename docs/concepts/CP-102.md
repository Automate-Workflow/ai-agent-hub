# CP-102: autonomy levels

![foundations](../../assets/icons/foundations.svg) ![beginner](../../assets/badges/beginner.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `foundations` | **Difficulty:** `beginner` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

Structured classification of how much independent planning, tool execution, and side-effect authority an AI system holds relative to humans.

## Why It Matters

Explicit autonomy tiers help engineering and governance teams match safeguards to real operational risk.

## How It Works

Taxonomies progress from human-assisted copilots and review workflows to supervised background runs and bounded autonomous agents.

## Example

Classifying a documentation drafter as human-review only while bounding automated cache restarts to supervised runs.

## Common Mistakes

Allowing marketing buzzwords like fully autonomous to override concrete permission and escalation specifications.

## Definition

The structured natural-language, multimodal, and schema payload supplied to a model at inference time to elicit a desired response or action.

## Why It Matters

Clear prompt design remains the fastest lever for controlling formatting, tone, tool selection, and reasoning quality.

## How It Works

Text instructions, few-shot demonstrations, context documents, and output constraints are tokenized together into the model context window.

## Example

Providing two annotated ticket classification examples alongside policy definitions to classify inbound support requests.

## Common Mistakes

Burying critical formatting rules inside verbose prose instead of putting unambiguous constraints near structured sections.

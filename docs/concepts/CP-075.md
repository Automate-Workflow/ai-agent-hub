## Definition

Verifying model completions against structural schemas, business invariants, policy classifiers, and citation presence prior to downstream execution.

## Why It Matters

Deterministic output checks stop malformed actions and policy violations before they impact external systems or users.

## How It Works

Validators inspect response syntax, allowed enum ranges, regex patterns, and grounded citations, triggering retries or fallbacks on failure.

## Example

Checking via Guardrails AI or Pydantic that a generated refund payload references a real active order ID.

## Common Mistakes

Executing side-effectful code or database queries solely because JSON syntax parsed without domain validation.

![models](../../assets/icons/models.svg) ![beginner](../../assets/badges/beginner.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

## Definition

Executing quantized foundation and small language models directly on user workstations, laptops, or private local hardware.

## Why It Matters

Local inference keeps confidential data on-device, eliminates per-token cloud egress fees, and works cleanly offline.

## How It Works

Runtimes such as Ollama or vLLM load quantized model weights onto local GPUs or unified memory with local OpenAI-compatible APIs.

## Example

Running local RAG assistance over private legal or medical workstation documents without external network transmission.

## Common Mistakes

Attempting complex long-context multi-agent loops on severely quantized tiny models without testing schema accuracy.

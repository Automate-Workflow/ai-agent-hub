## Definition

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines parametric language models with external non-parametric document retrieval at inference time to ground responses.

## Why It Matters

RAG reduces factual errors, supports private or recent knowledge without constant retraining, and enables verifiable source citations.

## How It Works

A query searches indexed corpora using dense, sparse, or hybrid retrievers, then passes top ranked chunks into the generation prompt.

## Example

A policy QA assistant retrieving current HR handbook sections and quoting clause identifiers in its reply.

## Common Mistakes

Dumping poorly chunked noisy search hits into prompts without filtering irrelevant passages or verifying grounding.

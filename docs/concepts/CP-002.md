# CP-002: agentic AI

![foundations](../../assets/icons/foundations.svg) ![beginner](../../assets/badges/beginner.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `foundations` | **Difficulty:** `beginner` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

AI systems in which foundation models dynamically direct their own execution flow, intermediate reasoning, memory lookups, and tool invocations across multiple steps.

## Why It Matters

Agentic AI shifts software from fixed hard-coded branches to adaptive problem solving over open-ended digital environments.

## How It Works

Instead of executing a rigid pipeline, the model inspects goal progress after each observation and decides whether to call tools, ask humans, or conclude.

## Example

A customer operations system that investigates order logs, verifies refund policies, and issues a partial credit without pre-scripted branches.

## Common Mistakes

Granting unrestricted autonomy when a deterministic workflow chain would achieve higher reliability at lower cost.

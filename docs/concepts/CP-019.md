# CP-019: prompt chaining

## Definition

Decomposing a complex task into sequential model steps where each step consumes and transforms the validated output of the prior stage.

## Why It Matters

Breaking large prompts into smaller focused stages improves accuracy, inspectability, and schema compliance over monolithic prompts.

## How It Works

Step one extracts entities or outlines, optional deterministic validators inspect intermediate output, and step two synthesizes the final artifact.

## Example

First extracting contract clauses into structured JSON, checking mandatory dates, and then generating a risk summary.

## Common Mistakes

Chaining too many sequential steps for latency-sensitive tasks where a single structured step would suffice.

# CP-129: constitutional and regulatory compliance

![business](../../assets/icons/business.svg) ![intermediate](../../assets/badges/intermediate.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `business` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

Aligning agent deployment lifecycles, documentation, logging, risk tiers, and human oversight with legal AI governance frameworks.

## Why It Matters

Public regulations and international management standards impose transparency and oversight duties on high-risk automated systems.

## How It Works

Organizations inventory agent risks, document model limits, enforce approval gates, preserve audit logs, and track post-market incidents.

## Example

Documenting human oversight controls and bias checks for an HR screening support agent.

## Common Mistakes

Treating legal compliance purely as an afterthought post-deployment rather than an engineering design requirement.

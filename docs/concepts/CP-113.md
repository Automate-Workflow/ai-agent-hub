# CP-113: reasoning models

![models](../../assets/icons/models.svg) ![intermediate](../../assets/badges/intermediate.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `models` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

Language models specifically trained via reinforcement learning to allocate hidden or structured test-time compute before answering.

## Why It Matters

Extended inference-time reasoning boosts complex planning, verification, code synthesis, and mathematical consistency.

## How It Works

Models generate structured internal deliberation chains exploring tradeoffs and catching errors prior to emitting external text or tools.

## Example

Invoking a reasoning model to verify concurrency race conditions in a complex distributed pull request.

## Common Mistakes

Deploying slow expensive reasoning models on trivial formatting or routing lookups where fast models excel.

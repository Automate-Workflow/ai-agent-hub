# CP-012: visual understanding and perception

![models](../../assets/icons/models.svg) ![intermediate](../../assets/badges/intermediate.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `models` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

The ability of multimodal models and agents to interpret screenshots, charts, scanned documents, diagrams, and camera frames alongside text.

## Why It Matters

Visual perception enables GUI computer use, invoice OCR, UI debugging, and physical robotics without brittle text-only adapters.

## How It Works

Vision encoders project image patches or video frames into the shared token embedding space processed by attention layers.

## Example

An inspector agent comparing rendered browser screenshots against layout guidelines to spot clipped text.

## Common Mistakes

Assuming OCR alone captures spatial layout cues needed to click small UI buttons or interpret plotted axes.

## Definition

Models and agents capable of jointly processing and generating multiple modalities across text, images, audio, and video within one architecture.

## Why It Matters

Native multimodality eliminates brittle external translation pipelines between screenshots, spoken dialogue, and documents.

## How It Works

Modality encoders map non-text tokens into unified Transformer attention layers producing text, tool calls, or audio frames.

## Example

A customer warranty agent inspecting uploaded product defect photos alongside spoken explanations.

## Common Mistakes

Assuming multimodal inputs bypass injection risks when adversarial instructions can be hidden inside images or audio.

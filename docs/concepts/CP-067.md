# CP-067: programmable dialogue rails

**Category:** `safety_and_security` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

Declarative conversational flow rules and domain-specific policies defining permitted dialogue paths, disallowed topics, and canned fallback behaviors.

## Why It Matters

Explicit dialogue rails constrain public-facing conversational bots to approved workflows and regulatory disclosures.

## How It Works

Rail engines such as NeMo Guardrails match user intents against canonical flows written in Colang before invoking generative tools.

## Example

Enforcing that a healthcare FAQ chatbot redirects medical diagnosis requests to verified emergency disclaimers.

## Common Mistakes

Writing overly rigid dialogue rails that break natural multi-turn follow-up questions.

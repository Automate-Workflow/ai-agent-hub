## Definition

Neural voice synthesis turning agent responses into natural, expressive streaming speech audio with low playback latency.

## Why It Matters

Low-latency synthesis makes phone and accessibility assistants feel conversational rather than stilted.

## How It Works

Streaming neural vocoders emit incremental audio frames sentence by sentence while handling pause markers and pronunciation.

## Example

Converting concise spoken confirmation prompts into streaming audio over WebRTC connections.

## Common Mistakes

Synthesizing unformatted markdown syntax, raw JSON, or long URLs verbatim aloud to listeners.

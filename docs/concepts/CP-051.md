## Definition

Decentralized multi-agent collaboration where peers pass tasks, share signals on a board, or negotiate locally without strict central control.

## Why It Matters

Decentralized topologies adapt naturally to exploratory parallel workloads or peer-to-peer specialist routing.

## How It Works

Autonomous peers read shared event channels or message queues, apply local interaction rules, and hand off work directly.

## Example

Multiple research agents sharding repository modules in parallel and reporting discrepancies on a shared board.

## Common Mistakes

Using unconstrained swarm loops on regulated financial workflows that demand auditable deterministic supervision.

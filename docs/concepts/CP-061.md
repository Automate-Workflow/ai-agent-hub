# CP-061: chain of thought

![reasoning](../../assets/icons/reasoning.svg) ![beginner](../../assets/badges/beginner.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `reasoning` | **Difficulty:** `beginner` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

Prompting or training language models to generate intermediate step-by-step reasoning traces before emitting a final conclusion or action.

## Why It Matters

Explicit intermediate steps unlock stronger performance on multi-step arithmetic, logic, code analysis, and policy application.

## How It Works

Generating explicit reasoning tokens expands sequential compute budget prior to selecting final output tokens.

## Example

Prompting a troubleshooting agent to list hypothesis checks step by step before picking a remediation command.

## Common Mistakes

Confusing plausible-sounding written reasoning steps with mathematically faithful internal verification.

## Definition

Combining lexical keyword search such as BM25 with dense vector similarity search via score fusion or reciprocal rank fusion.

## Why It Matters

Hybrid search captures both exact identifiers like error codes or part numbers and broad semantic intent.

## How It Works

Two parallel retrieval branches score documents lexically and semantically, normalize scores, and merge lists before reranking.

## Example

Locating specific regulation section identifiers via BM25 while semantically matching policy descriptions.

## Common Mistakes

Relying solely on dense vectors for queries containing exact SKU strings, CVE identifiers, or legal citations.

## Definition

Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) converting spoken audio waveforms into timestamped transcripts for agent processing.

## Why It Matters

Accurate multilingual ASR underpins voice customer service, clinical note dictation, and call analytics agents.

## How It Works

Acoustic models such as Whisper map mel-spectrogram features into multilingual subword transcripts robust to accent and noise.

## Example

Transcribing multilingual hotel front-desk calls cleanly before policy lookup and reservation confirmation.

## Common Mistakes

Sending raw unpunctuated noisy transcripts directly into brittle keyword rules without speaker turn tracking.

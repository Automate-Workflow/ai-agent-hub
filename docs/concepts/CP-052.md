## Definition

Using language and vision models with strict schemas to extract structured fields, dates, tables, and obligations from unstructured business documents.

## Why It Matters

Structured extraction accelerates lease review, claims intake, invoice matching, and onboarding while enabling deterministic validation.

## How It Works

Documents are parsed cleanly, segmented, processed via structured-output extraction calls, and checked against validation schemas.

## Example

Pulling renewal dates, escalation caps, and break clauses from commercial real-estate leases into structured database records.

## Common Mistakes

Extracting fields from multi-page contracts without page provenance pointers for human verification.

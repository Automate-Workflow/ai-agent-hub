# CP-010: voice and audio interaction

![interfaces](../../assets/icons/interfaces.svg) ![intermediate](../../assets/badges/intermediate.svg) ![active](../../assets/badges/active.svg)

**Category:** `interfaces` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

Real-time conversational interfaces combining audio capture, speech understanding, low-latency turn detection, and synthesized spoken output.

## Why It Matters

Voice unlocks hands-free support, hospitality, and accessibility workflows where typing latency would break natural interaction.

## How It Works

Streaming transport protocols such as WebRTC or WebSockets carry bidirectional audio frames to multimodal realtime models or cascaded STT-LLM-TTS pipelines.

## Example

A hotel concierge voice line streaming multilingual audio over WebRTC to confirm room service requests.

## Common Mistakes

Failing to handle user barge-in interruptions cleanly when streamed audio playback overlaps new speech.

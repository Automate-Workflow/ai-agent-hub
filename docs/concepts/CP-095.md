## Definition

Training a compact student model to reproduce the reasoning quality, tool choices, or soft outputs of a larger frontier teacher model.

## Why It Matters

Distillation delivers near-frontier task accuracy inside low-latency, lower-cost, or edge-deployable models.

## How It Works

Verified high-confidence trajectories from a strong teacher model are filtered by evaluators and used to fine-tune a small student.

## Example

Distilling complex ticket classification traces from a large model into a fast small model for real-time routing.

## Common Mistakes

Distilling unverified flawed teacher outputs directly into student weights, locking in bad habits.

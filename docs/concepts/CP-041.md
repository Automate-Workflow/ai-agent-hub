**Category:** `memory_and_knowledge` | **Difficulty:** `intermediate` | **Confidence:** `high` | **Status:** `active` | **Verified:** `2026-11-11`

## Definition

A graph-structured store representing typed entities and explicit multi-hop relationships for structured traversal alongside text search.

## Why It Matters

Knowledge graphs excel at multi-hop dependency queries, temporal fact updates, and entity disambiguation where flat chunks struggle.

## How It Works

Extractors map entities and predicates into nodes and edges queried via graph traversal queries or hybrid Graph-RAG pipelines.

## Example

Tracing service ownership dependencies across microservices, owners, and recent incident tickets.

## Common Mistakes

Building complex graph ontologies without clear retrieval query patterns or entity-resolution deduplication.
